How much to trust this

  • Survivorship bias. The universe is today’s winners. Companies that fell out of the index are missing, which makes buying dips look better than it was.
  • Clustered events. Many events fall in the same selloffs (2020, 2022). Results excluding those periods are shown separately.
  • Fundamentals are not point-in-time. The quality-filtered version uses today’s fundamentals, so it is “indicative” only.
  • Variants tried: logged in trials.csv. The best variant is a hypothesis, not a finding.
  • Taxes and FX are ignored.
Dip
Fell at least the minimum drop over the chosen window. Defaults: 1 day ≤ −4%, 5 days ≤ −7%, or ≥ 12% below the 20-day high.
Quality score
Share of six checks passed: ROE ≥ 12%, net margin ≥ 8%, debt/equity ≤ 150%, positive free cash flow, positive revenue growth, market cap ≥ 20B. Needs at least three checks with data.
Falling knife
Price below a falling 200-day average and at least 35% under the 52-week high. A long slide, not a one-off drop.
RSI
14-day Relative Strength Index, 0–100. Under 30 means the stock has fallen fast relative to its recent moves.
Vol ×
Today’s volume divided by the average of the previous 20 days.
Sources
Live runs use OpenBB with a Yahoo Finance fallback. Fundamentals are cached for 7 days and can be stale or wrong. Verify them before you rely on them.
Not investment advice
This is an educational research shortlist. A drop is not a reason to buy. Read the news and filings to judge whether the cause is temporary.
